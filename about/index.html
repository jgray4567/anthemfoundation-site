About The Anthem Foundation

Through the power of music, bridges of trust and understanding are created, relationships and cultures are strengthened, and people unite with contributions of creativity and harmony.

Music is the international language of peace and understanding among nations, and the ANTHEM Foundation was created with this in mind. Our goal is to foster good will and create greater awareness and better understanding among people of different culture sand backgrounds, thereby uplifting and unifying the human spirit.

Our goal is to achieve this by planning and delivering educational, entertainment and music cultural exchanges and musical tours in the United States and among countries of the world.

Affiliations

The ANTHEM Foundation affiliates with various organizations to help build greater understanding between diverse cultures, to build stronger relations between nations, and to unify goodness among people of different countries around the world.
