Our VISION:  To bridge cultures, strengthen relations between nations, and unify goodness within the human spirit through the power of music.

Mission:
Using the universal language of music, ANTHEM plans and organizes educational, entertainment and music cultural exchanges and tours in the United States and among countries of the world to foster good will, create greater awareness andbetter understanding among people of different cultures and backgrounds, and to uplift and unify the human spirit.
