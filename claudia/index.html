Claudia Brant

Songwriter and Producer

Throughout her career, she has received 9 Latin Grammy and Grammy nominations, she was awarded the Latin Grammy in 2009 (Song of the Year with “Aquí estoy yo”), the GRAMMY in 2019 (Best Latin Pop Album with Sincera), was named ‘Latina Songwriter of the Year’ by SESAC for three consecutive years (2007-2009) and ASCAP ‘Latina Songwriter of the Year’ in 2012 and 2015. Her song, “No Me Doy Por Vencido”, written with Luis Fonsi, was named “Song of the Decade” by Billboard Magazine and she was inducted into the Latin Songwriter’s Hall of Fame in 2016.
Some of the most important artists around the world have recorded her songs, such as: Camila Cabello, Christina Aguilera, Jessie Reyez, Ricky Martin, Laura Pausini, Alejandro Sanz, Pedro Capó, Ednita Nazario, Diego Torres, John Legend, Enrique Iglesias, Luis Fonsi, La Ley, La Santa Cecilia, Soledad Pastorutti, Marc Anthony, Becky G, Jennifer López, David Bisbal, Pedro Fernández, Alejandro Fernández, Rosario Flores, Luz Casal, Reik, Calibre 50, Sergio Dalma, Noel Shajris, Thalía, Antonio Orozco, Antonio Carmona, Carlos Santana, Barbara Streisand, Josh Groban, Paula Fernandes, Michael Bublé, Leslie Grace, Morat, Victor Manuelle, Luis Enrique, Ximena Sariñana, Pablo López, Ricardo Montaner, Pandora, Il Volo, 5th Harmony, The Tenors, Demi Lovato, Tim McGraw, Girls’ Generation, and many more.
Her songs have been interpreted in multiple languages including Spanish, English, French,Italian, Korean and Portuguese, earning Claudia numerous international Gold and Platinum record award recognitions for her work and multiple #1 hits across the globe.Claudia constantly collaborates with different producers and composers including: BrunoMars, Dianne Warren, David Foster, Eduardo Cabra, Humberto Gatica, Sebastian Krys, Jayson DeSuzio, George Noriega, Danny Elfman, JulioReyes Copello, Walter Affanassieff, Steve Diamond, Desmond Child, Chris DeStefano, Brett James, Mark Portmann, Marco Marinangeli, Cheche Alara, Julia Michaels and Rafa Sardina to name a few.Claudia was appointed ‘West Coast VP’ for The Latin Songwriters Hall of Fame and is a National Trustee Board Member for The Recording Academy (LA Chapter, NARAS).
LATEST RELEASES include Solo album release, Manuscrito Deluxe; a remastered version containing 8 songs from the original album Manuscrito and new single “Hojas del viento” with Nahuel Pennisi, “Juntos”which was Claudia’s first hit in Argentina and “No me doy por vencido 2021”, a new version of her iconic hit, rewritten by Claudia and Luis Fonsi. •Upcoming songs by: Monsieur Periné, Noel Shajris, Elsa y Elmar, Jay de la Cueva, La Santa Cecilia, Ximena Sariñana, Victoria La Mala, Chiquis Rivera and Techy Fatule.
